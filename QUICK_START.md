# Quick Start Guide - Shopify Advertorial Page

## What is an Advertorial?

An advertorial is a piece of content that reads like an editorial article but includes promotional elements for products. It's designed to educate and inform while naturally leading readers toward making a purchase.

## 5-Minute Setup

### 1. Upload Files (2 minutes)

In your Shopify theme editor, upload these files:

```
templates/
├── page.advertorial.liquid
└── page.advertorial.json

sections/
├── advertorial-header.liquid
├── advertorial-main.liquid
├── advertorial-products.liquid
└── advertorial-cta.liquid

assets/
└── advertorial.css
```

### 2. Create Your Page (1 minute)

1. Go to **Online Store → Pages → Add page**
2. Title: "The Ultimate Guide to [Your Product Topic]"
3. Template: Select "page.advertorial"
4. Click **Save**

### 3. Customize Content (2 minutes)

1. Click **Customize** on your new page
2. Update the headline and hero image
3. Add products to the products section
4. Update the CTA button link
5. Click **Save**

Done! Your advertorial page is live.

## Content Template

Use this structure for your advertorial:

### Introduction
- Hook: Start with a relatable problem
- Promise: What will the reader learn?

### The Problem
- Describe the pain point in detail
- Show empathy and understanding
- Use statistics or research if available

### The Discovery
- How you found the solution
- Research or testing process
- Why it's different from alternatives

### How It Works
- Explain the mechanism/approach
- Use simple, clear language
- Include benefits and features

### Social Proof
- Customer testimonials
- Expert endorsements
- Scientific backing

### Product Showcase
- Present the product naturally
- Focus on value, not just features
- Show pricing and deals

### Call-to-Action
- Clear next step
- Address objections
- Provide guarantee/risk reversal

## Examples of Good Advertorial Headlines

- "I Tried 27 [Products] — Here's What Actually Works"
- "The [Product] That Dermatologists Don't Want You to Know About"
- "Why Everyone Is Switching to [Product] in 2026"
- "A Brutally Honest Review of [Product]"
- "The Science Behind [Product]: Does It Really Work?"

## Content Blocks Cheat Sheet

### Text Block
Use for: Main paragraphs, explanations, storytelling

### Image Block  
Use for: Product photos, before/after, infographics

### Quote Block
Use for: Testimonials, expert opinions, statistics

### List Block
Use for: Benefits, features, steps, comparisons

### Product Block
Use for: Featuring specific products with buy buttons

## Tips for Maximum Conversions

✅ **DO:**
- Write in first person ("I discovered...")
- Use real examples and specific details
- Include prices and save amounts
- Show social proof throughout
- Make CTAs clear and visible

❌ **DON'T:**
- Make it too salesy or pushy
- Use excessive hype or false claims
- Hide that it's promotional content
- Ignore mobile optimization
- Forget to proofread

## Legal Requirements

**Important**: Always comply with advertising disclosure requirements:

1. **FTC Guidelines**: Clearly disclose affiliate relationships or sponsorships
2. **Add Disclaimer**: Include at bottom: "This page contains promotional content"
3. **Honest Claims**: Only make claims you can substantiate
4. **Clear Labeling**: Make it clear this is an advertorial, not pure editorial

Add a disclaimer section at the bottom:

```
Disclaimer: This article contains affiliate links. We may earn a commission 
if you make a purchase through these links at no additional cost to you.
```
